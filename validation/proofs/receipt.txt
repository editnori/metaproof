Notes on what the formula proofs do not cover
  P03's "Q <= df gives tau2 = 0" is DerSimonian-Laird only. REML can give tau2 > 0 with Q < df
  (SYNTHETIC y = (0,1,2), v = (0.5,0.1,10): Q = 1.80 < 2, tau2 = 0.149, reported I2 = 20.7%).
  P04 proves the Q-based I2. Random-effects rows of pooled.csv report tau2/(tau2 + s2),
  which equals the Q form only at the DL tau2; E02 checks both forms against the engine.
  P07 proves the approximate J. The engine uses the exact gamma form; E03 checks it.
  P10 proves only that a split keeps the total n. Split comparisons still share control
  participants and are correlated (Handbook v6.5 23.3.4, 6.2.9). The engine default is
  combine, several reported between-group MDs against one control are refused, and
  multi-period crossovers are excluded; E07 checks the arm-level paths.
  The Bend laws are over Nat and are not proofs of the real-valued formulas or of the engine.
